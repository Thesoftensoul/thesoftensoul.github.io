Align Your Energy Series from The Soften Soul

This is for you if:

  1. You were taught to mask your feelings, or never allowed to fully express them.
  2. You avoid your emotions so often that you've lost touch with what you're actually feeling.
  3. You struggle with boundaries and overextending to others.
  4. You feel anger, frustration, resentment, or guilt for not living authentically.

That's a sign your heart chakra is imbalanced.

In this session, we'll learn about the heart chakra to help you come back into contact with what's underneath the thoughts, the reactions, and the triggers.

  • How to identify a difficult emotion with curiosity, compassion and kindness
  • How this shows up in your relationships, with yourself and others
  • The benefits of a balanced heart chakra and new choices you can make
  • A practice you can cultivate and fold into your daily living

All you need is a quiet space, something to write with (optional), and an open mind.
